Summary
One Tap to Connect, Whatever the Wallet
The connect button opens one sheet: the wallets found on the device first, then the rest. A returning user is reconnected silently. Every error names the wallet and the next action. The sheet is one component, used on every screen that needs a wallet; no screen may build its own.
Requirements
Priorities agreed at the design review of 11 Sep 2026.

R5The sheet remembers the last wallet used, per deviceCould
Interface
Button
Label "Connect"; once connected, the address shortened to six and four characters, the chain as a dot.
Sheet
Wallet name and icon on one row each, 56 high; a section label above the second group; no search.
Error
Two lines under the row: what happened, then one action. Never a modal, never red on its own.
