02
Client
Atlas, a Wallet for People Who Hold One Token(fictional client)
Atlas came with a whitepaper, a token and a deadline: a wallet in the stores before the token listing. No screens, no team, no name for the app. The brief was to make the wallet simple enough that a first-time holder could receive, hold and stake in one session, and to ship it in twelve weeks.

What we did
Weeks 1 to 3 · Product
Interviews with twelve holders, one flow on paper, the onboarding cut from nine screens to four.
Weeks 4 to 9 · Build
React Native, one design system in Figma and in code, the staking flow tested with the protocol's testnet from week five.
Weeks 10 to 12 · Release
Store listings, the audit fixes, a beta with two hundred holders, and the launch on listing day.
Outcome
Client analytics, 90 days after launch; figures rounded.

What we would change
Start the store review in week eight, not ten; the two rounds of review cost a week we had not planned.
Keep the weekly demo. Every cut that made the product simpler was decided on a Friday, in front of the client.
